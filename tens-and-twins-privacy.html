Tens & Twins Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tens & Twins: Number Puzzle (the "Game"), an Android game published on Google Play by Fis GA Studio ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use it. The Game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It has no user accounts, and we don't run our own servers to collect your data. The Game is free to play and shows ads through Google AdMob. It has no in-app purchases.

The short version

  • No sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device, and in your Android backup if you use it.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device data such as your advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, scores, statistics, settings, daily puzzle history, daily gifts, achievements, trophies, unlocked color themes and earned bonus hints on your device. We don't receive this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup and copy it to a new phone. That backup is stored by Google under your account.

When you choose to share a result, the Game passes a short text to Android's share menu and you decide where to send it. We don't receive what you share.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Ad breaks: short full-screen ads between classic levels, only after you have played for a while and never during a move.
  • Banner: a small ad below the game on the home screen, the daily calendar, the trophies and the level-complete card, never while you play a board.
  • Rewarded videos: optional videos that you choose to watch for one hint after your free hints run out, for one more add in a classic level (up to two per board), or to double your daily gift.

Ads are requested only after Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) allows it where you live. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the Game automatically collects the following information and sends it to Google:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Interactions with the Game and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and, where permitted, to personalize ads. Google processes it under its own policies. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before your data is used for personalized ads, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message. You can review or change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • U.S. states with privacy laws: where required, the same Privacy choices option lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you'll still see ads, but they'll be less tailored to you.

4. How we use information

We use the limited information available to us only to:

  • run the Game;
  • show the ads that keep the Game free;
  • find and fix problems, and prevent fraud;
  • reply when you contact us; and
  • meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent for personalized ads, on the need to provide the Game, on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and secure, and on our legal obligations.

5. Sharing and transfers

We don't sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only with:

  • Google (AdMob), to show and measure ads, as described above; and
  • public authorities, when the law requires it.

Some privacy laws, such as those of certain U.S. states, treat the use of advertising identifiers for targeted ads as "sharing" or a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out at any time using the choices in section 3. Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using its own safeguards for international transfers.

6. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We don't operate servers that store your personal information. These measures reduce risk but can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. A copy in your Android backup is managed by Google under your account settings.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to Google's retention policies.
  • If you email us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to answer you and keep basic records.

To ask us to delete information we hold about you, email us at the address below. We can't remotely erase data that exists only on your device.

8. Children

Tens & Twins is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game or by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because the Game has no accounts, we usually can't link data to you. For data held by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls. To make a request, email us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and doesn't use cookies or analytics. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
