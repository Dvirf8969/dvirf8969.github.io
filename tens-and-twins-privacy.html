Tens & Twins Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tens & Twins (the "Game"), an Android game published on Google Play by Fis GA Studio ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use it. The Game has no user accounts, and we don't run our own servers to collect your data. The Game is free to play, shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

The short version

  • No sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device data such as your advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, scores, settings and unlocked items locally on your device. We don't receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including full-screen ads and optional rewarded video ads. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the Game automatically collects the following information and sends it to Google:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Interactions with the Game and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and, where permitted, to personalize ads. Google processes it under its own policies. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

3. Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before your data is used for personalized ads, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message. You can review or change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the Game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you'll still see ads, but they'll be less tailored to you.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases in the Game are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive your payment card or bank details. Google Play shares with us limited transaction information needed to deliver and support your purchase, such as the order number, the item purchased, the price, the date and your country.

5. How we use information

We use the limited information available to us only to:

  • run the Game and deliver the items you buy;
  • show the ads that keep the Game free;
  • find and fix problems, and prevent fraud;
  • reply when you contact us; and
  • meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent for personalized ads, on the need to provide the Game and the items you buy, on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and secure, and on our legal obligations.

6. Sharing and transfers

We don't sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only with:

  • Google (AdMob), to show and measure ads, as described above;
  • Google (Google Play), to process purchases; and
  • public authorities, when the law requires it.

Some privacy laws, such as those of certain U.S. states, treat the use of advertising identifiers for targeted ads as "sharing" or a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out at any time using the choices in section 3. Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using its own safeguards for international transfers.

7. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We don't operate servers that store your personal information. The limited purchase records available to us are kept in our Google Play Console account, which is protected by Google's account security.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to Google's retention policies.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, and in our Play Console, for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes.

To ask us to delete information we hold about you, email us at the address below. Because the Game has no accounts, please include your Google Play order number if your request is about a purchase.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we'll take steps to delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because the Game has no accounts, we usually can't link data to you. For data held by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls. To make a request, email us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and doesn't use cookies or analytics. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
