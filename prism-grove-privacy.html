Prism Grove: Light Puzzles Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Grove: Light Puzzles (the "Game"), an Android game published on Google Play by Fis GA Studio ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use it. The Game is made for players of all ages. It has no user accounts, and we don't run our own servers to collect your data.

The short version

  • The Game asks for an age group, not a birth date, and keeps the answer on your device.
  • Under 18 or age unknown: no ads, no purchases and no rating requests. The advertising and purchase services aren't started.
  • Adults can choose optional rewarded videos (Google AdMob) and one-time purchases (Google Play).
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.

1. Age choice and children

The Game is made for players of all ages. When you first open it, it asks for an age group: under 13, 13-17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. It doesn't ask for a birth date. The choice is stored on your device and isn't sent to us.

For anyone under 18, or whose age is unknown, the Game doesn't start its advertising, advertising-consent, in-app review or Google Play Billing services. These players see no ads and no purchases, get unlimited free hints and play the free content. Paid content is available only in adult mode. Changing from the adult setting closes the Game so those services stop; the new setting applies when the Game is opened again.

Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us. We don't ask children to email us personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress, stars, settings, hint allowances, daily gifts, achievements, collected light colors, daily puzzle history and age choice are saved on your device to run the Game and remember your choices. There is no Prism Grove account, login, server of ours or separate analytics service. App backup and device transfer of this data are turned off.

This data stays until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it. Doing so removes your local progress and settings. It doesn't delete Google's purchase records.

3. Advertising for adult players

Adults can choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded video for one hint, for two bonus hints after a win, or to double a daily gift. The Game has no banners and no full-screen ad breaks. Google's consent tool, the User Messaging Platform, manages the advertising choices that apply where you live, and ads are requested only after it allows them.

Once ads are allowed, the advertising software may load a video before you choose to watch it, and choosing not to watch doesn't undo that earlier processing. Depending on your settings and choices, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or other identifiers, interactions with the Game and with ads, and diagnostic information, to provide advertising, measure performance and prevent fraud. The Game doesn't request access to the Android advertising ID; this doesn't mean that no identifiers are used.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent and opt-outs: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some U.S. states), the Game shows Google's consent message. You can review or change your choice later from the privacy options in the Game's settings.
  • Watching is always optional: no puzzle requires an ad or a purchase.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center and in your Android settings.
  • Age group: choosing an age group under 18 turns ads and purchases off.

5. Purchases for adult players

Google Play handles optional one-time purchases of two extra chapters (Aurora Trails and Solstice Peaks) and a separate bundle of grove palettes. These are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. When you restore a purchase with the Google Play account that made it, the Game restores access to the content you bought, not your puzzle progress.

The Game receives purchase details, such as product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens and signatures, to verify, acknowledge and restore purchases. Verification happens on your device; we don't run a purchase server. We don't receive your payment card details. Google Play also shares with us, in our Play Console, limited transaction information such as the order number, the item purchased, the price, the date and your country. Refunds and restores go through Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Ratings for adult players

After several solved puzzles, the Game may ask an adult player once to rate it through Google Play's in-app review feature. Google Play shows and handles the review; the Game doesn't receive your rating or review text. This feature isn't used for players under 18 or whose age is unknown.

7. How we use information

We use the limited information available to us only to run the Game, deliver and restore what you buy, show the optional rewarded videos, find and fix problems, prevent fraud, reply when you contact us, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent for personalized ads, on the need to provide the Game and the items you buy, on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and secure, and on our legal obligations.

8. Sharing and transfers

We don't sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only with Google (AdMob), to show and measure ads for adult players; with Google (Google Play), to process purchases and reviews; and with public authorities when the law requires it. Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using its own safeguards for international transfers.

9. Security

Local storage uses Android's app isolation. Google's services use encrypted connections. These measures reduce risk but can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, Gmail processes your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send, and we use them only to answer you. Please send only what is needed, and never passwords or card details. Support emails are deleted from our mailbox, including its trash, within 90 days after your case is closed.

To ask for access to, correction or deletion of support information we hold, email dvir.fis@gmail.com. We may ask for enough detail to find the correspondence and confirm the request. We can't remotely erase progress that exists only on your device. Google's own retention, backups and deletion are governed by Google's policies; clearing the Game's data or contacting us doesn't erase them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw your consent at any time. For data held by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls. To make a request, email us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and doesn't use cookies or analytics. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the Game's data practices change and change the "Last updated" date above.
